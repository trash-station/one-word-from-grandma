One original word — the word a grandma would text about AI. The program writes that word to `/output/entry.txt`: "Doohickey".

## Why this word

"Doohickey" is the classic grandma word for a gadget she cannot name — which is exactly what she would text about AI. She does not know what the thing is called, so she calls it a doohickey, and the bafflement is the joke: warm, a little lost, and funny in a single word with no caption needed. It is tied to machines, not to any subject at all — a doohickey is what you call a gadget, a remote, a robot, the blinking box on the counter — and it is fresh where "robot", "Skynet" or "Siri" would be the obvious picks. Nine letters, instantly readable, and it sounds like her thumb-typing it to a grandchild.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
